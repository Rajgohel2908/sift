ACME GLOBAL CORP - ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY (REVISED 2024)

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the annual leave entitlements for all full-time regular employees at Acme Global Corp.

2. Annual Leave Entitlement
All full-time employees are entitled to 24 annual leave days per calendar year. Leave accrues on a monthly basis at the rate of 2 days per completed month of service.

3. Carry-Over and Expiration
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Any carried-over days must be utilized before March 31st of that year.

4. Approval Process
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance and approved by the department manager.
