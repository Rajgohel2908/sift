ACME GLOBAL CORP - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK: WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE

1. Standard Working Hours
The standard work week consists of 40 hours, scheduled Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break each day.

2. Flexible Working Arrangements
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Employees may adjust their starting and ending times with managerial approval, provided they fulfill the required 40 weekly hours.

3. Remote Work Guidelines
Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per week subject to team performance and manager agreement.
